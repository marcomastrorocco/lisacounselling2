About Lisa

Master of Counselling · Edith Cowan University · ACA Registered Counsellor

I'm an ACA Registered Counsellor with a Master of Counselling and more than 15 years of experience across Queensland Government, health and community services — supporting adults, young people and families through some of life's most difficult experiences.

Spes is the Latin word for hope. It reflects the belief that even through difficulty, healing, possibility and meaningful change can remain within reach.

Healing begins with feeling safe, understood and respected.

Qualifications & Professional Background

  • Master of Counselling — Edith Cowan University
  • Registered Counsellor — Australian Counselling Association (ACA)

Over more than 15 years across Queensland Government, health and community services, I have worked alongside people affected by complex trauma, domestic and family violence, coercive control, sexual violence, victimisation and crime, grief and loss, mental health challenges, alcohol and other drug concerns, and housing instability. My professional experience includes counselling, crisis intervention, psychosocial and risk assessment, safety planning, case coordination and trauma-informed therapeutic support.

A bicultural practice

As someone who has migrated myself, I understand the emotional weight of building a life in a new country — questions of identity, belonging and living between cultures. I warmly welcome migrants, expatriates and culturally diverse individuals and families, with counselling available in English and Italian.

I believe healing begins with feeling safe, understood and accepted. My aim is a therapeutic relationship where you feel empowered to explore your experiences, reconnect with your strengths, and move towards meaningful and lasting change.
