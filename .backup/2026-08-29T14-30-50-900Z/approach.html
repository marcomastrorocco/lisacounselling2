My Approach

Every person brings their own story, experiences and strengths. My approach is integrative and flexible, with counselling tailored to your individual needs rather than shaped by a single therapeutic model.

At your pace. In your way. With support every step of the way.

My practice is grounded in

  • Trauma-informed care
  • Person-centred therapy
  • Attachment-informed practice
  • Strengths-based and recovery-oriented practice
  • Culturally responsive care
  • Evidence-based therapeutic approaches
  • Somatically informed practice

Depending on your needs, therapy may incorporate

  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)
  • Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)
  • Solution-Focused Therapy (SFBT)
  • Person-Centred Therapy
  • Mindfulness and grounding strategies
  • Psychoeducation
  • Attachment-informed interventions

Above all, I believe counselling should be collaborative, respectful and tailored to what is most meaningful for you.

What to expect

Beginning counselling can feel like a significant step. Our first session is an opportunity to talk about what has brought you here, what is important to you and what you would like to be different. There is no expectation to tell your whole story at once — we work at a pace that feels safe and manageable for you.

Together, we may explore the impact of difficult experiences while also recognising your strengths, what is already working, and the changes you would like to make. Counselling can support you to develop practical ways of managing challenges, build confidence and self-compassion, strengthen relationships and communication, process grief and change, and take meaningful steps towards the life you want to create.
