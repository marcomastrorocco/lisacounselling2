My Approach

Every person brings a unique story, strengths and way of understanding the world. For this reason, I use an integrative and flexible approach, adapting therapy to your individual needs rather than following a single therapeutic model.

At your pace. In your way. With support every step of the way.

My practice is grounded in

  • Trauma-informed care
  • Person-centred therapy
  • Attachment-informed practice
  • Strengths-based and recovery-oriented practice
  • Culturally responsive care
  • Evidence-based therapeutic approaches
  • Somatically informed practice

Depending on your needs, therapy may incorporate

  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)
  • Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)
  • Solution-Focused Therapy (SFBT)
  • Motivational Interviewing (MI)
  • Mindfulness and grounding strategies
  • Psychoeducation
  • Attachment-informed interventions

Above all, I believe counselling should be collaborative, respectful and tailored to what is most meaningful for you.

What to expect

Beginning counselling can feel like a significant step. Our first session is simply an opportunity to talk about what has brought you here and what you hope to achieve. There is no expectation to tell your whole story at once — we move at a pace that feels safe and manageable.

Together we may work towards understanding the impact of difficult experiences, developing emotional regulation and coping strategies, improving relationships and communication, building confidence and self-compassion, processing grief and change, and reconnecting with your values and goals.
