My Approach

Every person brings a unique story and way of understanding the world. I use an integrative, trauma-informed approach — adapting therapy to you, rather than fitting you to a single model.

At your pace. In your way. With support every step of the way.

The foundations

My practice is grounded in trauma-informed care, person-centred and attachment-informed practice, strengths-based and recovery-oriented approaches, culturally responsive care and somatically informed practice — recognising that trauma is held not only in our thoughts and emotions, but in the body.

Depending on your needs, therapy may draw on Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Solution-Focused Brief Therapy, Motivational Interviewing, mindfulness and grounding strategies, psychoeducation, and nervous-system regulation techniques. These approaches are always collaborative and guided by your comfort and preferences.

What to expect

Beginning counselling can feel like a significant step. Our first session is simply an opportunity to talk about what has brought you here and what you hope to achieve. There is no expectation to tell your whole story at once — we move at a pace that feels safe and manageable.

Together we may work towards understanding the impact of difficult experiences, developing emotional regulation and coping strategies, improving relationships and communication, building confidence and self-compassion, processing grief and change, and reconnecting with your values and goals.
