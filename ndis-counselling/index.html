NDIS counselling

Trauma-informed counselling shaped around your goals, needs and pace.

Counselling centred on you

Your experiences, strengths and goals are at the heart of our work together. If you are self-managed or plan-managed, counselling may be funded through your NDIS plan when it relates to your individual goals.

Sessions offer a safe, respectful and non-judgmental space where you can feel heard, explore what is affecting you and develop practical strategies for everyday life.

How counselling can help

Counselling can help you understand and manage difficult emotions, build confidence, strengthen relationships and navigate change, grief or trauma. Together, we can develop practical strategies that support your wellbeing, independence and everyday life.

Support is shaped around your individual needs and NDIS goals, at a pace that feels right for you.

Taking the first step

Self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants are welcome to enquire online. If you are unsure whether counselling can be funded through your plan, please contact SPES Counselling before submitting an enquiry.

Available services

The counselling services offered to NDIS participants are the same as the existing SPES COUNSELLING services. NDIS access involves a different funding, administration and onboarding process; it does not require separate versions of the same counselling service pages.

  • Trauma and abuse
  • Anxiety, depression and overwhelm
  • Grief, loss and life transitions
  • Relationships and family
  • Migration and cultural adjustment
  • Alcohol and other drug concerns

How the NDIS process works

  1. Submit an enquiry so we can understand what support you are looking for.
  2. Confirm suitability and funding in relation to your goals and plan-management arrangement.
  3. Complete an initial consultation to discuss your needs, preferences and goals.
  4. Finalise the service agreement and documentation before sessions begin.
  5. Begin counselling sessions in person or via secure telehealth, as appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral?

We can discuss referral requirements and suitability when you enquire.

Can counselling be funded through my NDIS plan?

Funding depends on your individual plan and goals. We will discuss this with you before counselling begins.

Which plan-management arrangements are accepted?

SPES COUNSELLING welcomes eligible self-managed and plan-managed participants.

How do invoicing and documentation work?

Funding, invoicing, service agreements and any required documentation are confirmed during the onboarding process.

Are sessions available by telehealth?

In-person and secure telehealth sessions are available. Sessions can be provided in English or Italian.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Please share only the information needed to discuss suitability and next steps. Do not include detailed health information in the form.
