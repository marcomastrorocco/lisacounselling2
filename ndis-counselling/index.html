NDIS counselling

Trauma-informed counselling shaped around your goals, needs and pace.

Support that starts with what matters to you

If you are self-managed or plan-managed, NDIS counselling may be available where it aligns with the goals in your plan. Sessions provide a calm, respectful space to explore what is happening in your life and build practical strategies at a pace that feels right for you.

How counselling can help

Counselling can support emotional wellbeing, confidence, relationships, life transitions, anxiety, grief and the impacts of trauma. Together, we can identify your strengths, work towards meaningful goals and develop tools that support daily life.

Getting started

Before booking, please get in touch to discuss your plan, goals and whether this service is a good fit for you. Sessions are available in Brisbane and via secure telehealth across Australia, in English and Italian.
