From surviving to healing. From healing to living.

Whether you are living with the effects of trauma, experiencing anxiety or depression, navigating grief, relationship or parenting difficulties, or adjusting to life in a new country — you don't have to have all the answers before reaching out.

My approach is trauma-informed, evidence-based and integrative, recognising the connection between mind, body and nervous system. Together we work at a pace that feels safe and respectful, building on your strengths towards recovery and lasting change.
