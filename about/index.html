About Lisa

I'm an M.A.C.A registered Counsellor with a Master of Counselling and more than 15 years of experience across Queensland Government, health and community services — supporting adults, young people and families through some of life's most difficult experiences.

Healing begins with feeling safe, understood and respected.

Throughout my career I have worked alongside people affected by complex trauma, domestic and family violence, coercive control, sexual violence, victimisation and crime, grief and loss, mental health challenges, alcohol and other drug concerns, and housing instability. My professional background includes counselling, crisis intervention, psychosocial and risk assessment, safety planning and trauma-informed therapeutic support.

As someone who has migrated myself, I understand the emotional weight of building a life in a new country — questions of identity, belonging and living between cultures. I warmly welcome migrants, expatriates and culturally diverse individuals and families, with counselling available in English and Italian.

I believe healing begins with feeling safe, understood and accepted. My aim is a therapeutic relationship where you feel empowered to explore your experiences, reconnect with your strengths, and move towards meaningful and lasting change.
